# OYYO Nano 1.0

**Everyday writing and planning** · Local text-model candidate

A compact starting point for emails, short briefs, checklists and structured answers. Useful when you want a general assistant without choosing a specialist.

## Suitable requests

Capabilities in the runtime catalog: chat, planning, structured-output. These are routing labels and intended tasks, not independent benchmark results. Provide the source facts, audience, constraints and desired format; OYYO can prepare a role-specific prompt.

> Draft a friendly customer email explaining a two-day shipping delay. Give a subject line and a clear next step.

## Limits and verification

Long documents and complex multi-step reasoning can exceed the practical capacity of this compact option.

This is a text model. It does not by itself generate images/video, transcribe audio, browse the web, execute code, publish posts or export documents. Those features require the corresponding OYYO tool, speech pack or connected service. Output length and supplied context are controlled by runtime settings; a maximum usable context is not certified in this registry.

OYYO names identify product configurations. They do not assert that the underlying weights were originally trained by OYYO. Required rights, attribution and provenance records remain applicable; see [licensing](../LICENSE.md) and [provenance policy](../provenance/README.md).
