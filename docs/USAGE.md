# Ask naturally and get a usable result

You do not need to name an architecture or write a technical system prompt. State the goal, supply reliable inputs, describe constraints and say what you want back. OYYO can prepare the specialist instructions.

## Marketing

> Plan a two-day launch for our reusable notebook. Budget: $600. Audience: university students. Give me three complete social drafts, a budget table adding to $600, a daily checklist and next actions. Do not publish anything.

Evaluate writing/planning candidates and the marketing workflow. Review facts and prices. A “send to social” next action needs a real authorised publishing connection before anything is posted.

## Coding

> This function fails when the list is empty. Here is the code and error. Propose the smallest fix, explain the cause and give tests for empty, one-item and repeated-item inputs.

Evaluate Code, Core or Focus candidates. Ask the host's authorised coding tools to apply and test changes. A model saying “tests passed” does not constitute test execution.

## Finance

> Unit cost is $16 and retail price is $99. Build a 100-unit launch scenario with a 15% discount and $600 marketing spend. Show revenue, direct cost and contribution before overhead. Mark missing taxes, fees and shipping as unknown.

Evaluate Reason, Focus Pro or an analysis-capable model. Use calculation tools to verify arithmetic and distinguish contribution from net profit.

## People and HR

> Draft a role description for a customer-success specialist and a structured interview guide based only on these responsibilities. Flag missing employment terms for our review.

Use HR templates and a writing-capable qualified model. Keep hiring decisions and jurisdiction-dependent policy review with the appropriate people.

## Research

> Compare the three supplied documents. Give a table of agreement, contradictions and unanswered questions, with source references. Separate evidence from your interpretation.

Use analysis-capable models with the relevant local sources. New information from the web requires an actual search tool; memory alone is not a current web search.

## Meeting notes

> Summarise this transcript into decisions and actions. Include timestamps, owners and deadlines where stated. Use “Not specified” for anything missing. Then translate the result into Italian.

In NotzUp, capture/import a supported short clip or edit/paste a transcript. Tiny ALL supplies speech-to-text; a qualified local text model supplies summaries and translation. Speaker labels can be edited manually. Check recognition before relying on the summary.

## Creative production

> Prepare three visual directions for a notebook launch: clean studio, warm lifestyle and neon cyberpunk. Keep our logo and product features consistent. Give a generation brief for each and ask me which to produce.

The text result is a brief. Producing an image or video requires the matching generation tool and its own execution settings.

## Project blueprint

> Create a project blueprint for launching our meeting app: objective, audience, requirements, work packages, owners, timeline, creative prompts, acceptance criteria and next actions. Mark assumptions and ask only for critical missing information.

Use the OYYO Blueprint template in the task library. Blueprints belong in OYYO's general workspace; NotzUp focuses on meeting and note work.

## A better follow-up

Tell OYYO exactly what to refine: “Expand the budget assumptions,” “Make the email warmer,” “Use the selected team,” or “Turn this approved outline into the final brief.” Saved profiles and custom templates help keep recurring work consistent without resending every instruction.
