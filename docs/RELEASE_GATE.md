# Official Model Release Gate

A candidate may be branded as an official OYYO model only after:

1. Upstream identity and revision are frozen.
2. License and redistribution rights are reviewed.
3. Artifact checksums are recorded.
4. CPU portability is tested for the intended tier.
5. Target accelerator paths are tested where claimed.
6. OYYO Benchmark qualification is complete.
7. Safety/security and malformed-input tests are complete.
8. Model card and known limitations are published.
9. Human acceptance review is complete.

Popularity, download count, benchmark marketing, or vendor claims are not sufficient.
