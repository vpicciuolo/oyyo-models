## How the catalogs fit together

**Models** provide inference capabilities. **Agents** define specialist responsibilities. **Teams** coordinate selected roles. **Tasks** describe the outcome and brief. **Benchmark** documents qualification methodology. **SDK** documents integration surfaces.

Catalog entries are not proof that an action ran, a model passed on every device or a service is available to every account. Refer to the relevant capability and qualification notes.
